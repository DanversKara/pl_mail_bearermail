# Android app

The BearerMail Android app: mail, calendar, contacts, notes and Drive, with home-screen
widgets. [Download it here](download.md).

## Install

1. On the phone, open the [download page](download.md) and tap **Download BearerMail
   for Android**.
2. Open the downloaded file. If Android says installs from this source are blocked, tap
   **Settings**, allow it for your browser, go back, and tap **Install**.
3. Open BearerMail and pair it with your server: on the web, go to **Settings → App
   passwords → Pair a device** and scan the code with the phone.

## What it does

- **Mail** — every mailbox on your server in one inbox, with push for new mail,
  one-time codes you can copy straight from the notification, and swipe actions.
- **Calendar** — day, week and month views, reminders (several per event: the night
  before, 30 minutes before, and so on) shown as notifications.
- **Contacts** — the same address book as the web, with photos and duplicate merging.
- **Notes** — Keep-style notes and checklists, with pictures, drawings, voice notes,
  reminders and sharing.
- **Drive** — your files, uploads, and share links; share any file into BearerMail
  Drive from other apps.
- **App lock** — a PIN in front of the app.

## Widgets

Long-press the home screen, open **Widgets → BearerMail**, and drag one out:

| Widget | Shows |
|---|---|
| Calendar agenda | Upcoming events as coloured cards, with icons for reminders, repeats and linked mail or notes |
| Calendar agenda (floating) | The same, with no background — the cards sit on your wallpaper |
| Calendar month | The month grid with the events in it |
| Notes | Your notes as cards, pinned first, with a + for a new one |
| Notes (floating) | The same, with no background |

Widgets update by themselves when something changes on the web or another phone.

## Updates

The app checks for a newer release and offers to install it. To check now, open the menu
and tap **Check for updates**.

## Things that bite

- **Widgets not updating.** Android may stop background work for apps it thinks you do
  not use. In the phone's settings, set BearerMail's battery use to **Unrestricted**.
- **No notifications.** Allow notifications for BearerMail when it asks, or later in the
  phone's settings under Apps → BearerMail → Notifications.
- **Voice notes ask for the microphone** the first time you record one, and never before.
